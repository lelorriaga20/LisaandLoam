Our story

Hi, I'm Lisa.

I have always loved a good organizational tool. There's a real pleasure in a place where all the important details live together — one page that holds the whole picture, so you can work smart instead of hard. It's how I've always liked to think, and it turns out it's the thing I most like to make.

I like things simple, too. Clean lines, quiet color, plenty of open space. A planner should feel like a calm surface to think on, not a wall of boxes waiting to be filled — and when a page is genuinely beautiful, you want to open it. A planner you want to open is a planner you'll actually use.

And I love to travel. Planning is half the joy of a trip, but only when the planning is about the adventure — the coast road, the little town you'd never have found, the morning you wake up somewhere new. Get the details settled in one calm place, and all that attention is free for the good part.

Loam is the rich, dark soil where things grow best — and that's the idea behind everything in the shop. Clear the clutter, and the good things — the trip, the goal, the ordinary day worth keeping — have room to take root.

Every Lisa & Loam design is built in a soft, earthy palette, tested on real devices and real trips, and refined until it's as practical as it is pretty. If it wouldn't earn a permanent spot on my own iPad, it doesn't go in the shop.

Thanks for being here — I hope something here makes your next plan feel like the start of the adventure.

— Lisa
